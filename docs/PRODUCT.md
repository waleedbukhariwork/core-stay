# Product

CodeCore is a mobile-first practice product for software engineers who want to stay current, stay sharp, and keep progressing.

The product loop is:

**Learn → Practice → Review → Reinforce → Track**

AI is underlying intelligence inside that loop. It is not the product positioning.

Phase 2 adds account creation, email verification, sign-in and session management after the product introduction. Phase 3 continues into Goals → Role → Experience → Tech Stack → Focus Areas → Daily Time → Learning Preferences. Progress is saved to the authenticated account after each step and resumes from backend state.

Phase 4 extends Diagnostic Intro through persisted questions, optional confidence, deterministic answer review and Diagnostic Complete. Phase 4A activates the immutable `engineering-baseline-v1` catalog with eight curated general-engineering questions. No runtime AI-generated content or evaluation is used. Starting Skill Profile, Engineering Health and personalized plans belong to Phase 5; learning, general practice and billing remain outside the current scope.
